JOB DESCRIPTION: HR EXECUTIVE (HUMAN RESOURCES)
===============================================

JOB OVERVIEW
------------
An HR Executive handles people-related work in an organisation: hiring, onboarding, employee records, attendance, payroll support, employee engagement and compliance with labour rules. The role connects employees with management.

KEY RESPONSIBILITIES
--------------------
1. Post job openings on job portals and social media and collect applications.
2. Screen resumes, schedule interviews and coordinate with hiring managers.
3. Conduct first-round interviews and prepare offer letters.
4. Onboard new employees: documents, joining formalities, induction and orientation.
5. Maintain employee records, files and HR database (HRMS).
6. Track attendance, leave and working hours, and share inputs for payroll.
7. Assist in salary processing, PF, ESI and other statutory compliance.
8. Organise employee engagement activities, training sessions and events.
9. Handle employee queries and basic grievances with confidentiality.
10. Support performance appraisal processes.
11. Manage exit formalities such as resignation, clearance and exit interviews.
12. Make sure company policies and labour laws are followed.

ELIGIBILITY / QUALIFICATIONS
----------------------------
- Graduation in any stream; BBA, MBA (HR), MSW or PG Diploma in HR preferred.
- Freshers can join as HR Trainee or Recruiter.

SKILLS REQUIRED
---------------
- Good communication and interviewing skills
- People handling, empathy and conflict resolution
- Knowledge of recruitment tools and job portals
- MS Excel, MS Word and HR software
- Basic knowledge of labour laws and payroll
- Organisation, confidentiality and honesty

WORKING CONDITIONS
------------------
- Office-based; occasional campus hiring drives or travel.
- Busy during hiring seasons, appraisals and payroll dates.

CAREER GROWTH
-------------
HR Trainee -> HR Executive -> Senior HR Executive -> HR Manager -> HR Head / Chief People Officer. Specialisations: Talent Acquisition, Payroll, Training & Development, Employee Relations.
