JOB DESCRIPTION: JBT TEACHER (JUNIOR BASIC TEACHER)
====================================================

JOB OVERVIEW
------------
A JBT Teacher teaches students of primary classes (generally Class 1 to 5) in government or private schools. The teacher builds the base of reading, writing, arithmetic and good habits in young children, and supports their overall growth in the early years of schooling.

KEY RESPONSIBILITIES
--------------------
1. Teach all primary-level subjects such as Hindi, English, Mathematics, EVS (Environmental Studies), and local language as per the school curriculum.
2. Prepare daily lesson plans, teaching notes and activity plans.
3. Use simple methods like stories, songs, games, charts and models to make learning enjoyable.
4. Teach basic reading, writing and number skills and track each child's progress.
5. Conduct regular tests, assignments and oral assessments, and maintain result records.
6. Maintain attendance registers, student records and report cards.
7. Keep discipline in class in a kind and positive manner.
8. Communicate with parents through meetings (PTM), and inform them about the child's progress and behaviour.
9. Take part in school events such as morning assembly, annual day, sports day and cultural programmes.
10. Support government programmes where applicable (mid-day meal supervision, enrolment drives, health checkups, census or election duty as assigned).
11. Help children with special needs and slow learners with extra attention.

ELIGIBILITY / QUALIFICATIONS
----------------------------
- Senior Secondary (10+2) with the minimum marks required by the state/board.
- Diploma in Elementary Education (D.El.Ed / JBT / BTC) or B.El.Ed, or other qualification recognised by NCTE.
- Qualified TET (Teacher Eligibility Test) of the state or CTET, as required for recruitment.
- Age limit and reservation rules as per the state recruitment notification.

SKILLS REQUIRED
---------------
- Patience and love for children
- Clear speaking and good handwriting
- Creativity in teaching (activity-based learning)
- Classroom management
- Basic computer knowledge (for records and digital teaching)
- Communication with parents

WORKING CONDITIONS
------------------
- Full-time job, usually 6 to 7 hours a day, 5 to 6 days a week.
- Posting may be in rural or remote areas in government service.
- Follows the school academic calendar with summer and winter vacations.

CAREER GROWTH
-------------
JBT Teacher -> Head Teacher / Center Head Teacher -> Block Elementary Education Officer -> other administrative education posts. A JBT can also complete B.Ed and move to higher teaching posts (TGT) through promotion or fresh recruitment.

HOW TO PREPARE (FOR APPLICANTS)
-------------------------------
- Complete D.El.Ed or B.El.Ed from a recognised institute.
- Prepare for TET (Child Development and Pedagogy, Language I, Language II, Mathematics, EVS).
- Watch state education department websites for recruitment notices.
