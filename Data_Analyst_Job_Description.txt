JOB DESCRIPTION: DATA ANALYST
=============================

JOB OVERVIEW
------------
A Data Analyst collects, cleans and studies data to find patterns and insights that help a business make better decisions. The analyst presents results through reports, dashboards and charts that managers can easily understand.

KEY RESPONSIBILITIES
--------------------
1. Collect data from databases, spreadsheets, websites and business systems.
2. Clean and prepare data by removing errors, duplicates and missing values.
3. Analyse data using Excel, SQL, Python or R to find trends and patterns.
4. Build dashboards and reports using Power BI, Tableau or Excel.
5. Present findings to managers and teams in simple language.
6. Work with business teams to understand their questions and goals.
7. Track key business metrics (sales, customers, costs, website traffic, etc.).
8. Create forecasts and basic statistical models when needed.
9. Check data quality and keep documentation of data sources and methods.
10. Suggest practical actions based on the data.

ELIGIBILITY / QUALIFICATIONS
----------------------------
- Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science, IT, Statistics, Maths, Economics, Commerce, Engineering, BCA/MCA or related field.
- Certifications or courses in data analytics are helpful.

SKILLS REQUIRED
---------------
- Advanced Excel (formulas, pivot tables)
- SQL for querying databases
- Python (Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib) or R
- Power BI / Tableau
- Statistics and basic maths
- Critical thinking and problem solving
- Clear communication and storytelling with data

WORKING CONDITIONS
------------------
- Office, hybrid or remote; works with business, IT and management teams.
- Deadlines around monthly or quarterly reporting.

CAREER GROWTH
-------------
Junior Data Analyst -> Data Analyst -> Senior Data Analyst -> Analytics Manager. Many analysts also move into Data Science, Machine Learning, Business Analysis or Data Engineering.

HOW TO PREPARE (FOR APPLICANTS)
-------------------------------
- Learn SQL, Excel and Python, then practise on real datasets.
- Build 2 to 3 portfolio projects with dashboards and case studies.
- Be ready to explain your analysis steps in interviews.
