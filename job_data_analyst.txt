DATA ANALYST
==================================================
Company    : BrightLeaf Analytics (fictional)
Location   : Remote (India)
Experience : 0-2 years
Job Type   : Full-time

ABOUT THE ROLE
Join our insights team to turn raw business data into clear
dashboards and recommendations.

RESPONSIBILITIES
- Clean, transform, and analyze large datasets
- Build dashboards in Power BI or Tableau
- Write SQL queries for ad-hoc reporting
- Present findings to non-technical stakeholders
- Track KPIs and flag anomalies

REQUIRED SKILLS
- SQL and Excel (pivot tables, VLOOKUP/XLOOKUP)
- Python (pandas, NumPy, matplotlib)
- Power BI or Tableau
- Good communication and problem-solving skills

GOOD TO HAVE
- Basic statistics
- A/B testing knowledge
- Experience with Google Analytics

QUALIFICATION
Bachelor's or Master's in Computer Applications, Statistics, or a
related field
