JOB DESCRIPTION: DATA ENTRY OPERATOR
====================================

JOB OVERVIEW
------------
A Data Entry Operator enters, updates and checks information in computer systems, spreadsheets or databases. The job needs speed, accuracy and attention to detail. Data entry operators work in offices, banks, hospitals, government departments, schools, e-commerce companies and BPOs.

KEY RESPONSIBILITIES
--------------------
1. Type and enter data from paper forms, scanned documents, invoices, registers or emails into computer software.
2. Update and maintain existing records in databases or Excel sheets.
3. Check entered data for mistakes and correct errors.
4. Verify information by comparing with the source documents.
5. Scan, upload, organise and store documents (digital and physical files).
6. Prepare simple reports and lists using MS Excel or other software.
7. Keep data confidential and secure; follow company data-protection rules.
8. Take regular backups of files as instructed.
9. Meet daily or weekly targets for speed and accuracy.
10. Report problems in the system or missing information to the supervisor.
11. Do other office tasks as needed, such as filing, photocopying or answering emails.

ELIGIBILITY / QUALIFICATIONS
----------------------------
- Minimum 10+2 (some jobs accept 10th pass); graduation is preferred for government and bank roles.
- Computer course or diploma (such as DCA, CCC or O Level) is an advantage.
- Typing speed commonly required: around 30 to 40 words per minute in English or Hindi, or 8,000 to 10,000 key depressions per hour for some jobs.

SKILLS REQUIRED
---------------
- Fast and accurate typing
- Good knowledge of MS Word, MS Excel and basic computer operations
- Attention to detail and patience
- Basic English and/or Hindi reading and writing
- Time management and ability to do repetitive work
- Honesty and respect for confidential data

WORKING CONDITIONS
------------------
- Desk job in an office, with a lot of time on the computer.
- Fixed shifts; work-from-home options exist in some private companies.
- Short breaks and good posture help avoid eye strain and back pain.

CAREER GROWTH
-------------
Data Entry Operator -> Senior Operator / Data Coordinator -> MIS Executive -> Office Assistant / Team Leader -> Data Analyst (with Excel, SQL and analytics skills). Government DEO roles can lead to promotions in the department.

TIPS FOR APPLICANTS
-------------------
- Practise typing daily using free typing tests.
- Learn Excel formulas (SUM, VLOOKUP, filters, pivot tables).
- Be careful of fake "online data entry jobs" that ask for registration fees; genuine employers do not charge money to hire you.
