JOB DESCRIPTION: CUSTOMER SUPPORT EXECUTIVE
===========================================

JOB OVERVIEW
------------
A Customer Support Executive is the first point of contact for customers. The executive answers questions, solves problems and helps customers by phone, email, chat or social media, making sure they have a good experience with the company.

KEY RESPONSIBILITIES
--------------------
1. Answer incoming calls, emails and chats from customers politely and quickly.
2. Understand the customer's problem and give correct information or solutions.
3. Handle complaints calmly and escalate difficult issues to seniors.
4. Record each customer interaction in the CRM or ticketing system.
5. Follow up with customers until the issue is fully solved.
6. Process orders, refunds, replacements, bookings or service requests as per policy.
7. Explain products, services, offers and prices.
8. Collect customer feedback and report common problems to the team.
9. Meet targets for response time, resolution time and customer satisfaction.
10. Keep product and policy knowledge updated.

ELIGIBILITY / QUALIFICATIONS
----------------------------
- Minimum 10+2; graduation preferred.
- Freshers are welcome; training is usually given by the company.

SKILLS REQUIRED
---------------
- Clear spoken and written communication (English and/or Hindi)
- Active listening, patience and politeness
- Problem solving and quick thinking
- Typing speed and basic computer skills
- Ability to stay calm under pressure and work in shifts

WORKING CONDITIONS
------------------
- Call centre, BPO, office or work-from-home; rotational shifts are common, including nights for international support.
- Target-based work with regular quality checks.

CAREER GROWTH
-------------
Customer Support Executive -> Senior Executive -> Team Leader -> Process Manager / Quality Analyst -> Operations Manager. Can also move to sales, account management or training roles.
