JOB DESCRIPTION: ACCOUNTANT
===========================

JOB OVERVIEW
------------
An Accountant records and manages the financial transactions of a business, prepares financial statements, handles taxes and helps the company control its money. Accountants work in companies, shops, banks, CA firms, schools and government offices.

KEY RESPONSIBILITIES
--------------------
1. Record daily transactions such as sales, purchases, payments and receipts in accounting software.
2. Prepare and check invoices, vouchers, bills and bank reconciliation statements.
3. Maintain ledgers, cash book and day book accurately.
4. Process payments to vendors and follow up on money due from customers.
5. Prepare monthly, quarterly and yearly financial reports (profit and loss, balance sheet).
6. Calculate and file GST, TDS and income tax returns as required.
7. Help with payroll, PF and ESI calculations.
8. Prepare budgets and track expenses against them.
9. Assist auditors and keep documents ready for audit.
10. Make sure accounting follows company policy and legal rules.
11. Spot errors, fraud risks or unusual transactions and report them.

ELIGIBILITY / QUALIFICATIONS
----------------------------
- B.Com / M.Com or equivalent; CA Inter, CMA or ICWA is an advantage.
- Experience with accounting software such as Tally, Busy or QuickBooks.

SKILLS REQUIRED
---------------
- Strong knowledge of accounting principles
- Tally Prime, MS Excel (formulas, pivot tables)
- Understanding of GST, TDS and income tax basics
- Accuracy, attention to detail and honesty
- Time management and ability to meet filing deadlines
- Basic communication and reporting skills

WORKING CONDITIONS
------------------
- Office job; busy during month-end, quarter-end, tax filing and financial year-end.

CAREER GROWTH
-------------
Accounts Assistant -> Accountant -> Senior Accountant -> Accounts Manager -> Finance Manager / CFO. With CA, CMA or MBA (Finance), accountants can move into auditing, taxation, financial analysis or consulting.
