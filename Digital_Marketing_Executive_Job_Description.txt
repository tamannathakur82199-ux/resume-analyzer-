JOB DESCRIPTION: DIGITAL MARKETING EXECUTIVE
============================================

JOB OVERVIEW
------------
A Digital Marketing Executive promotes a business or brand through online channels such as social media, search engines, email, websites and paid ads. The goal is to increase website traffic, build brand awareness and generate leads and sales.

KEY RESPONSIBILITIES
--------------------
1. Plan and run social media campaigns on Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, YouTube and X.
2. Create and schedule posts, reels, banners and short videos (with the design team).
3. Optimise the website and content for search engines (SEO), including keyword research.
4. Run and manage paid ads on Google Ads and Meta Ads, and control the ad budget.
5. Write content for blogs, emails, captions and ad copy.
6. Run email and WhatsApp marketing campaigns.
7. Track campaign results with Google Analytics, Search Console and platform insights.
8. Prepare weekly or monthly performance reports.
9. Reply to customer comments and messages and manage the brand's online reputation.
10. Study competitors and trending topics to improve campaigns.
11. Work with influencers or partners where needed.

ELIGIBILITY / QUALIFICATIONS
----------------------------
- Bachelor's degree in any stream (Marketing, Mass Communication, BBA, BCA, Commerce are common).
- A digital marketing course or certification (Google, Meta, HubSpot) is a plus.
- Freshers can apply with internships or personal project experience.

SKILLS REQUIRED
---------------
- SEO and social media marketing basics
- Google Ads and Meta Ads Manager
- Content writing and copywriting
- Canva or basic design/video editing
- Google Analytics and Excel for reporting
- Creativity, curiosity about trends and good communication

WORKING CONDITIONS
------------------
- Mostly desk-based; remote and freelance options are common.
- Fast-moving work with changing platform rules and trends.

CAREER GROWTH
-------------
Digital Marketing Executive -> Senior Executive -> Digital Marketing Manager -> Head of Digital / Marketing Manager. Specialisations include SEO specialist, performance marketer, content strategist or social media manager.
