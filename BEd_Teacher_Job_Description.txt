JOB DESCRIPTION: B.ED TEACHER (TGT / SECONDARY SCHOOL TEACHER)
================================================================

JOB OVERVIEW
------------
A B.Ed Teacher teaches students of middle, secondary and senior secondary classes (generally Class 6 to 12) in a specific subject such as Maths, Science, English, Social Science, Hindi, Computer Science or others. The teacher prepares students for school exams and board exams and guides them in academics and character building.

KEY RESPONSIBILITIES
--------------------
1. Teach the assigned subject according to the syllabus of the board (CBSE, State Board, ICSE etc.).
2. Prepare yearly plans, unit plans and daily lesson plans.
3. Deliver lessons using the blackboard, smart class, presentations, experiments and projects.
4. Set question papers, conduct unit tests, term exams and practical exams.
5. Check answer sheets, assignments and notebooks and give useful feedback.
6. Identify weak students and take remedial classes; encourage bright students with advanced work.
7. Maintain student attendance, marks registers and other records.
8. Act as a class teacher or house in-charge if assigned.
9. Guide students for career choices, Olympiads, competitions and science or art exhibitions.
10. Attend parent-teacher meetings and give regular progress updates.
11. Take part in staff meetings, training programmes and school activities.
12. Follow school rules and promote discipline and values among students.

ELIGIBILITY / QUALIFICATIONS
----------------------------
- Bachelor's degree in the relevant subject with minimum marks as required.
- B.Ed (Bachelor of Education) from a recognised institution.
- Qualified TET / CTET / State TET for government or many private schools.
- For senior classes (PGT): Master's degree in the subject plus B.Ed.

SKILLS REQUIRED
---------------
- Strong subject knowledge
- Clear communication and explanation skills
- Lesson planning and time management
- Classroom control and student counselling skills
- Use of technology: MS Office, Google Classroom, online tools, smart boards
- Patience, fairness and leadership

WORKING CONDITIONS
------------------
- Full-time, usually 6 to 8 hours a day with 6 to 8 teaching periods.
- Extra duty during exams, admissions and school events.
- Vacations as per the academic calendar.

CAREER GROWTH
-------------
TGT -> PGT -> Head of Department -> Vice Principal -> Principal. Teachers can also move into education administration, curriculum design, online teaching, content creation or coaching.

HOW TO PREPARE (FOR APPLICANTS)
-------------------------------
- Complete graduation and B.Ed, then clear TET / CTET.
- Practise demo lessons and keep updated on the NCERT syllabus.
- Prepare for interviews with a teaching demonstration and subject questions.
