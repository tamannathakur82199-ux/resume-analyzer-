JOB DESCRIPTION: GRAPHIC DESIGNER
=================================

JOB OVERVIEW
------------
A Graphic Designer creates visual content such as logos, posters, social media graphics, brochures, banners, packaging and website visuals. The designer turns ideas and messages into attractive, clear designs that match a brand.

KEY RESPONSIBILITIES
--------------------
1. Understand the client's or team's requirements and the target audience.
2. Design logos, posters, flyers, brochures, social media posts, ads and presentations.
3. Follow brand guidelines for colours, fonts and style.
4. Choose suitable images, typography and layouts.
5. Prepare designs for print (correct size, resolution, bleed) and for digital use.
6. Edit photos and create simple videos or animations if required.
7. Share drafts, take feedback and make revisions on time.
8. Work with marketing, content and web teams.
9. Organise and store design files properly.
10. Keep up with design trends and new tools.

ELIGIBILITY / QUALIFICATIONS
----------------------------
- Graduation or diploma in Graphic Design, Fine Arts, Multimedia, Animation or any stream with a strong design portfolio.
- A portfolio of good work matters more than a degree for most employers.

SKILLS REQUIRED
---------------
- Adobe Photoshop, Illustrator, InDesign; Canva; Figma (for UI work)
- Understanding of colour theory, typography and layout
- Creativity and visual sense
- Time management and ability to take feedback
- Basic knowledge of video editing (Premiere Pro, CapCut) is a plus

WORKING CONDITIONS
------------------
- Office, remote or freelance; long hours on the computer.
- Tight deadlines for campaigns and client projects.

CAREER GROWTH
-------------
Junior Designer -> Graphic Designer -> Senior Designer -> Art Director / Creative Director. Related paths: UI/UX Designer, Motion Graphics Artist, Brand Designer.
